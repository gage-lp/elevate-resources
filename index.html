Privacy & Terms

Confidentiality and ownership

This system and its contents — documents, videos, templates, training material and scripts — are the confidential property of Elevate Realty Group and are provided solely for use by current ERG team members in the course of their work.

You may not copy, download, forward, publish or reproduce any of this material outside the team, or continue to use it after you leave ERG. Access is granted for as long as you are an active member of the team and may be withdrawn at any time.

Third-party material

Not everything here is owned by ERG. Some material is licensed from third parties and remains subject to the terms of those licences. Anything sourced from a real estate board, an MLS system, a brokerage, or a paid template or stock library carries its own restrictions on copying and redistribution, and those restrictions apply on top of everything else on this page. If you are unsure whether a particular item can be shared or reused, ask before you use it.

Acceptable use

  • Use your own login. Do not share your credentials with anyone, inside or outside the team.
  • Do not upload client personal information, signed deal documents, or anything containing a client's private details to this system. It is not the system of record for transaction files.
  • Download material only when you need it for your work, and do not keep copies after you leave the team.
  • Report a lost or stolen device, or any sign that your account has been accessed by someone else, to ERG Client Care right away.

Personal information we store

To run this system we store your name, your email address, the time you last signed in, and the text of any content requests you submit. We use it only to give you access, to operate the resource board, and to respond to your requests. We do not sell it or use it for marketing.

Where that information is stored

This system uses Supabase, a third-party hosting provider, to store its data and files. Depending on how the service is configured, that information may be stored or backed up on servers located outside Canada, where it could be subject to the laws of another country.

You can request written information about ERG's policies and practices regarding service providers outside Canada, and ask any questions about how your personal information is collected, used, disclosed or stored, by contacting the Director of Client Care at clientcare@elevaterealtygroup.ca.

Access, corrections and concerns

You can ask to see the personal information this system holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or raise a privacy concern, by contacting the Director of Client Care at the address above. Personal information held by ERG is governed by Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act.

Changes

These terms may be updated from time to time. Continuing to use the system after a change means you accept the current version.
